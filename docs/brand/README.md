# TallyBear brand assets

The mark pairs a white bear and a milk-tea brown bear holding an open ledger. Use `public/brand/tallybear-logo.png` for the README, and the 192/512 pixel variants for app manifests. The optional minimal theme keeps its plain ledger icon.

The mark was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using `public/characters/together.png` as a visual reference. It is not an official Bubu & Yier logo. See [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for artwork attribution and licensing boundaries.

## Generation prompt

Create the finished TallyBear app brand logo featuring BOTH reference characters: the soft white bear with dark round ears and the soft milk-tea brown bear. They must be equally prominent, cheek to cheek above a tiny open cream ledger with a single lavender checkmark. Keep their sweet minimal dot eyes, tiny w mouths and blush cheeks, reference-like gentle thin cocoa outlines. Elegant compact emblem built from two rounded heads and small paws, NOT full bodies. White bear on left, milk-tea brown on right. Cozy contemporary app icon, restrained flat vector-style fills, dusty lavender detail. A plain solid warm ivory background #FAF7F2, square, generous equal margins. NO dark thick borders, NO large generic teddy bear, NO orange fur, NO pink costume, NO leaf or duck, NO decorative border, NO gradients, NO texture, NO lettering or text, NO checkerboard. Make it look like a refined recognizable two-bear brand symbol, charming but simple enough at 48 pixels.
