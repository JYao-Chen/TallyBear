## English

TallyBear 1.5 brings everyday bookkeeping, family money movements and financial analysis into one tool-using AI conversation, with editable confirmation cards and independent personal/family assets.

### AI that helps complete the task

- Prepare entries, recurring subscriptions, quick-entry presets, budgets, allocations, installment purchases and repayments through natural language.
- Upload transfer screenshots to the conversation; look up real family members and wallets, and prepare family movement or receipt-confirmation cards.
- Edit categories, wallets, amounts, scene fields and itemized purchases directly on a card before confirming.
- Keep cards anchored to their originating turn. Manage and delete conversation/report history.
- Select the current book, several books or all accessible books for analysis; deduplicate linked financial events across books.
- Faster delivery of streamed responses, an expanding composer, integrated attachments and improved desktop/mobile layouts.

### Family movements, without double-counting

- Member transfers, gifts, shared-wallet contributions, AA expense shares and settlement, loans and partial repayments.
- The sender selects their funding wallet and recipient; the recipient chooses their own destination wallet. Each party's private wallets remain private.
- Each participant chooses a personal display book, with a remembered default. View incoming/outgoing records and confirmation status in its fund-movement section.
- Reassign or remove your display link without changing balances or the other person's view. Existing movements can be assigned later.
- Check duplicate payment references and similar transfers; repeated confirmation does not post twice. Internal movements do not become household income or expenses.

### A clearer accounting model

- Personal and shared assets are independent of books. Moving or reusing an expense preserves the payment account and one underlying money movement.
- Wallet management includes concise names, deletion/archiving rules and merge handling; categories support editing, deletion and ordering.
- Credit and BNPL purchases, partial/early repayments, future installment changes and repayment reversal.
- Separate principal transfers, interest/fees and cost allocation. Allocation spreads cost without creating another payment or changing debt.
- Structured transport, dining, shopping and grocery scenes, reusable quick-entry presets and relevant historical examples.

### Better receipts and exploration

- Editable product rows and corrected item-type normalization in AI cards.
- Partial-checkout evidence can be reviewed without inventing discounts; useful notes replace fulfillment status and missing-field commentary.
- Transaction/create/update timestamps and device-time fallback for missing transaction times.
- Multicolor charts and transaction drilldown for supported category/time dimensions.
- Pagination across growing lists, icon-rich selectors, responsive date controls and corrected nested-dialog scroll behavior.

### Upgrade

Back up PostgreSQL, attachments and `.env`. Check out `v1.5.0`, retain the existing language/currency and keys, and apply the bundled schema before starting the new web and worker processes. Docker Compose runs initialization as part of startup.

Existing family movements are not automatically assigned to a private book. Use **Display book** on a movement to select your view; subsequent selections become the default. The counterpart chooses independently.

## 简体中文

TallyBear 1.5 将日常记账、家庭资金往来与财务分析整合到可调用工具的 AI 对话中，通过可编辑确认卡片完成操作，并将个人／家庭资产与账本归属分开管理。

### 从“问问账本”到交互式完成操作

- 用自然语言准备账单、周期订阅、常用一笔、预算、费用分摊、分期购买和还款。
- 在对话中上传转账截图，查询真实家庭成员和钱包，生成往来或收款确认卡片。
- 直接修改卡片中的分类、钱包、金额、场景和商品明细，核对后确认保存。
- 卡片跟随原始对话轮次；历史对话和报告可删除。
- 自选当前、多个或全部可访问账本，跨账本分析对关联事件去重。
- 优化流式传输、自适应输入框、图片附件与电脑／手机对话布局。

### 家庭往来，同一笔钱不重复记支出

- 支持成员转账、红包、共同入金、AA 份额与结算、借款及部分还款。
- 付款方选择自己的钱包和收款成员，收款方自行选择到账钱包；双方私人钱包互不可见。
- 双方分别选择个人展示账本并记住默认值，在资金往来区域查看转入、转出和到账状态。
- 移动或取消自己的展示关联，不改变余额或对方的视图；旧记录也可补选账本。
- 核对重复流水与相似转账，重复确认不重复入账，内部流动不增加家庭收支。

### 资产、消费与跨期费用更清楚

- 个人／家庭资产独立于账本；移动或复用账目保留付款账户，同一资金事件只计算一次。
- 精简钱包名称，完善删除、归档与合并；分类可编辑、删除和排序。
- 支持信用卡／白条等分期购买、部分还款、提前结清、调整后续各期及撤销误记还款。
- 区分本金转账、利息手续费与费用分摊，分摊不重复扣款，也不改变负债。
- 统一交通、餐饮、购物、买菜场景字段；支持快捷预设与相关历史样例参考。

### 识图与前端体验改进

- 商品明细可直接增删改，修复 AI 明细类型导致的错误缺项提示。
- 对截图未展示的结算内容采用可确认流程，不编造优惠；备注保留有价值的用途信息。
- 展示交易、创建和修改时间，缺失交易时间时使用设备时间。
- 丰富图表配色，支持按分类或时间维度下钻账目。
- 长列表分页、图标化选择器、响应式日期组件，以及嵌套弹窗关闭后的滚动修复。

### 升级

先备份 PostgreSQL、附件与 `.env`，切换到 `v1.5.0`，保留原语言、币种与密钥，执行随版本提供的数据库初始化更新，再启动新 Web 和 Worker。Docker Compose 会在启动流程中完成初始化。

历史家庭往来不会自动归入私人账本。点击记录的 **展示账本** 选择自己的展示位置；对方独立选择，后续记住默认值。
