# TallyBear version history

English · [简体中文](version-history.zh-CN.md)

TallyBear is the application name. The current version is **2.0**.

## 2.0 · Financial memory and cross-book workflows
September 27, 2026

The focus moved from recording individual purchases to managing their context across books.

- Activities group travel and event spending across books, with family participation, types, budgets, archives and reactivation.
- Analysis brings charts, search and transaction details together, with year, month, week, day and custom ranges.
- Personal categories follow the user. Refund lookup spans accessible books and supports multiple refunds and cashback.
- Financial memory connects products, preferences and source records. Exact, keyword and optional vector retrieval support field-level draft suggestions and conflict review.
- Assistant conversations persist on send; management tools prepare confirmation cards for supported operations.
- Receipt cards separate products, discounts, fees and processing hints. Quantity × unit price recommends subtotals without overwriting independent amounts.
- Profile contains full-page memory management; family movements have a dedicated entry workspace. Desktop and mobile documentation now have separate English and Chinese galleries.

[2.0 release details](releases/v2.0.0.md)

## 1.5 · Conversational actions and shared finance
September 16, 2026

The assistant gained editable action cards, and personal wallets became independent of book organization.

- Confirmation cards cover entries, budgets, subscriptions, allocations, installments and family movements.
- Family transfers, gifts, contributions, expense settlements and loans keep each member's wallet private.
- Installment management adds partial and early repayment, schedule adjustment and reversal.
- Quick-entry presets and spending contexts reduce repeated input.
- Conversations support scope selection, chart drilldowns and saved reports.
- Growing lists use pagination, with improved desktop and mobile navigation.

[1.5 source](https://github.com/JYao-Chen/TallyBear/tree/v1.5.0)

## 1.0 · First public release
September 14, 2026

The first public version established the core personal and family bookkeeping workspace.

- Private and shared books, families, accounts and wallets.
- AI text and receipt recognition, editable items and duplicate suggestions.
- A tool-using financial assistant with charts, reports and background jobs.
- Budgets, recurring bills, refunds and cost allocation.
- Receipt attachments, personal photos and transaction search.
- Chinese and English deployment options, four currencies, bear and minimal themes, Docker Compose installation.
